Executive Summary

Abstract: Automated Job Scam Detection

Summary of the machine learning framework engineered for high-recall scam classification

SLIDE 02 / 12
The Proliferation Problem

Online employment fraud has surged by over 118% year-over-year, targeting job seekers through deceptive advertisements on major hiring platforms.

  • Harvesters exploit emotional urgency to steal PII, SSNs, & bank accounts.
  • Manual moderation teams are overwhelmed by the volume of daily submissions.
The FraudLens Solution

FraudLens introduces an end-to-end textual intelligence pipeline synthesizing 5 critical job post sections into a high-dimensional feature space.

  • Combines 10,000 sublinear TF-IDF n-grams (unigrams + bigrams).
  • Employs cost-sensitive regularized optimization to offset rare class bias.
Phase II Milestone Impact

Validated on the EMSCAD benchmark (17,880 listings), achieving outstanding detection sensitivity without heavy computational requirements.

  • 90.75% Scam Recall (Catches 157 out of 173 test scams).
  • 98.83% ROC-AUC Score with transparent word attribution.
💡
Core Takeaway for 2nd Review: FraudLens successfully proves that combining sublinear term-frequency scaling with balanced inverse class weights resolves severe class imbalance (~4.84%) while maintaining instant inference latency (<15ms) suitable for production gatekeeping.

Context & Justification

Scope & Motivation: The Scalability Crisis

Analyzing why modern recruitment scams bypass manual moderation and how FraudLens intervenes

SLIDE 03 / 12
Surge of Sophisticated Scams

Modern recruitment fraud has shifted from crude spam to elaborate corporate impersonation campaigns:

  • Identity Theft (PII Harvesting): Fraudulent forms asking for government IDs, SSN, and banking credentials during initial screening.
  • Advance-Fee & Fake Equipment Fraud: Sending counterfeit checks for "home office setup" and requesting wire refunds.
  • Cryptocurrency & Money Laundering: Recruiting unwitting applicants as "financial transfer agents" or "payroll clerks".
  • Platform Degradation: Erosion of user trust on legitimate hiring boards and career portals.
Why Manual Review Fails at Scale

Human moderation pipelines suffer from critical structural bottlenecks in high-volume environments:

  • Volume Asymmetry: 50,000+ postings ingested daily per portal vs. limited human review capacity.
  • Review Latency (4–24 hours): Scammers harvest victim submissions during the first 2 hours before a manual flag occurs.
  • Cognitive Fatigue: Subtle linguistic deceit is frequently missed by human moderators skimming thousands of words.
  • Static Keyword Flaws: Scammers bypass word filters using unicode hacks, evasion synonyms, or vague phrasing.

Domain Foundation

Introduction: The Online Recruitment Ecosystem

Vulnerabilities in digital hiring portals and the strategic role of Natural Language Processing

SLIDE 04 / 12
Digital Hiring Channels & Attack Surfaces

Digital employment boards (LinkedIn, Indeed, Monster, RemoteOK) have democratized recruitment but opened frictionless vectors for bad actors. Anyone with a low-cost account can publish convincing, unvetted job listings reaching millions of job seekers globally within minutes.

The Job Seeker Vulnerability Vector

Job seekers—especially recent graduates, laid-off professionals, and remote gig-workers—are psychologically primed to trust authoritative-looking postings. When presented with flexible hours and competitive salaries, candidates readily submit sensitive personal data and comply with off-platform communication requests.

The Role of NLP in Scam Detection

Unstructured text contains rich psychological and stylistic markers that distinguish genuine corporate hiring from predatory schemes:

  • Semantic Discrepancies: Mismatch between high compensation and vague skill requirements.
  • Linguistic Pressure Cues: Excessive urgency ("urgent hire", "immediate start", "wire transfer").
  • Structural Anisotropy: Sparse company profile fields paired with generic description templates.
⚡ NLP transforms raw text into statistical discriminators before human exposure.

Core Innovation

Proposed Work: Novelty & Linguistic Extraction

Sublinear term scaling combined with inverse class-weighting to isolate deception signals

SLIDE 08 / 12
Dual-Pillar Algorithmic Strategy
Pillar 1: Sublinear TF Scaling
wf = 1 + log(tf)  [if tf > 0]
Dampens the score of repeated spam phrases without penalizing natural variance in document length.
Pillar 2: Balanced Inverse Loss Weighting
w_j = N / (2 × N_j)  →  w_scam ≈ 10.32 vs w_legit ≈ 0.53
Penalizes false negatives 19.6× more heavily, pushing the linear decision boundary towards high scam recall.
